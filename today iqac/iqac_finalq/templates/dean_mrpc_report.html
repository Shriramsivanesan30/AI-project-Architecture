Vision and Mission of the Institute

Vision

We develop globally competitive workforce and entrepreneurs

Mission

Dr. Mahalingam College of Engineering and Technology, Pollachi endeavors to impart high quality; competency based technical education in Engineering and Technology to the younger generation with the required skills and abilities to face the challenging needs of the industry around the globe.

This institution is also striving hard to attain a unique status in the international level by means of infrastructure, state-of-the-art computer facilities and techniques.

Vision and Mission of the Office of Dean Research & Innovation

Vision

To become a globally recognized hub of pioneering research and innovation, driving transformative advancements in engineering and technology for the betterment of society.

Mission

  • ➢ Foster research excellence and innovation by identifying potential IPR, offering financial support for filing, facilitating revenue sharing from consultancy collaborations, and rewarding faculty through fair incentives.
  • ➢ Strengthen research interest groups, empower the student research council, and publish the R&I newsletter to promote and celebrate achievements in the domain of research and innovation.
  • ➢ Disseminate knowledge through the publication of research, knowledge-sharing sessions, seminars, faculty development programs, workshops, inter- and intra-college technical symposiums, conferences, and open-house exhibitions.
  • ➢ Offer state-of-the-art research facilities and resources to facilitate interdisciplinary research projects, secure research funding, conduct research with industry partners, and carry out PhD works.
  • ➢ Review PhD scholars' progress, department R&I activities, ongoing externally funded projects, in-house R&D projects, and Institute Innovation Council activities.

Office of Dean - Research & Innovation

Research Initiatives

Research & Collaboration Groups

  1. Facilitation and integration of interdisciplinary Research Interest Groups (RIGs)
  2. Promotion and institutional support of Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) activities
  3. Guidance, coordination, and strengthening of Student Research Council (SRC) activities

Faculty & Scholar Development

  1. Strategic enhancement of full-time research scholar intake
  2. Strengthening the implementation and operational effectiveness of the Research Faculty Scheme
  3. Organization of Knowledge Sharing Sessions (KSS) and advanced research seminars
  4. Promotion of faculty participation in NPTEL–SWAYAM certification programmes
  5. Enhancement of faculty engagement in Faculty Development Programmes (FDPs)
  6. Facilitation of government recognition and empanelment for faculty consultancy services
  7. Strengthening structured reward and recognition mechanisms for faculty research contributions
  8. Augmentation of research support through enhanced access to print and e-journal resources

Research Review & Monitoring

  1. Institution-wide coordination of all NIRF parameters and strategic initiatives to improve national ranking performance
  2. Institutionalization of periodic and structured reviews of departmental R&I activities
  3. Monitoring and evaluation of the periodic progress of Ph.D. faculty members
  4. Review and monitoring of externally funded, in-house research, and consultancy projects
  5. Coordination of affiliation, ranking, accreditation, and approval-related activities through IQAC (Nov 2025 to Mar 2026)
  6. Devising faculty appraisal mechanisms and coordinating, verifying, and ensuring their systematic implementation

Research Incentives & Support

  1. Implementation of structured incentive administration and performance evaluation frameworks under RI1000, PRO600, SRO, and IP100
  2. Processing and administration of contingency grants to strengthen research infrastructure
  3. Establishment and advancement of the IPR Cell through in-house capacity building with adequate technical support
  4. Facilitating technology transfer, commercialization, and industry linkage for institutional research outcomes
  5. Enabling faculty-led start-ups for innovation grants and consultancy through mentoring, coordination and registration support
  6. Coordination of approval, monitoring, and review of faculty in-house R&D projects
  7. Administration of revenue-sharing mechanisms for faculty consultancy activities

Research Communication & Publication

  1. Publication of periodic faculty- and department-level R&I performance review reports
  2. Development, maintenance, and regular updating of the monthly research dashboard
  3. Periodic updating of research-related content on the institutional website
  4. Publication and circulation of the institutional R&I newsletter
  5. Proactive engagement with funding agencies and industries for opportunity identification, faculty assignment, proposal monitoring, and structured handholding of grant submissions
  6. Development and publication of standardized research process handbooks
  7. Design, implementation, and maintenance of R&I process workflows, templates, and formats
  8. Creation, maintenance, and periodic updating of the IRINS profile
  9. Preparation of comprehensive annual documentation of institutional R&I activities
